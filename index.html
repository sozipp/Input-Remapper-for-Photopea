> remap anything into photopea’s own shortcuts

■ What it does

Turn any key, mouse button or scroll gesture into any of Photopea’s shortcuts. Press Insert > get Free Transform (Ctrl+Alt+T). Scroll with Alt > zoom. Remap everything, your way.

Works ONLY on photopea.com — your OS and the rest of the web stay untouched. Browser shortcuts are blocked so you never hijack them by accident.

■ Features

  • Key to key remapping, with any modifier combos
  • Mouse buttons & wheel gestures as triggers
  • 60+ one-click Photopea presets, searchable
  • Record the trigger — just press it
  • Live on/off switch, syncs across your browser
  • Local & private — nothing leaves your device
