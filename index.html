Photopea shortcuts,
your way.

Turn any key, mouse button or scroll gesture into any of Photopea’s shortcuts. Press Insert → get Free Transform (Ctrl+Alt+T). Alt+scroll → zoom. Works only on photopea.com — your OS and the rest of the web stay untouched.

What you get

  • Key-to-key remapping with any modifier combos
  • Mouse buttons & wheel gestures as triggers
  • 60+ one-click, searchable Photopea presets
  • Record a trigger — just press it
  • Live on/off switch, synced across your browser
  • Browser shortcuts blocked so nothing gets hijacked
